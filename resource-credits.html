Anatomy and imaging

The hip-anatomy, biomechanics, manual-therapy anatomy and synthetic-imaging preview images derive from the AnatomyTool Open 3D Anatomical Model, led by Leiden University Medical Center and collaborators, with Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D lineage. The anatomy and these anatomy-derived views retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes include source-owned structure extraction, teaching transforms and screenshot framing. Original contributor credits.

The region pictures and turntable animations on the reference library are renders of simLAB’s joint and skeleton models, which derive from the same AnatomyTool Open 3D Anatomical Model. They are adaptations and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: bones only, recoloured, relit, framed, turned through 360° and, for the whole skeleton, mirrored from one side. The home page tiles are also adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0: the knee with its muscle, nerve and vessel layers revealed in turn, simLAB’s supine hip flexion range-of-motion and horizontal-canal BPPV examinations, and simPACS X-ray, CT and MRI views of the same left shoulder. Each region and topic page opens on its own short loop: screen captures of simLAB’s joint mechanics, movement, eye and vestibular labs playing the resource the tile links to, and the shoulder with its layers revealed in turn. The anatomy in those loops is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Changes: framed, sped up to loop, the joint lab’s background replaced with the card gradient, and the lab controls hidden.
