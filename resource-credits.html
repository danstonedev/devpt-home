Anatomy and imaging

The hip-anatomy, biomechanics, manual-therapy anatomy and synthetic-imaging preview images derive from the AnatomyTool Open 3D Anatomical Model, led by Leiden University Medical Center and collaborators, with Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D lineage. The anatomy and these anatomy-derived views retain CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes include source-owned structure extraction, teaching transforms and screenshot framing. Original contributor credits.

The region pictures and turntable animations on the reference library, and the skeleton on its home page, are renders of simLAB’s joint and skeleton models, which derive from the same AnatomyTool Open 3D Anatomical Model. They are adaptations and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: bones only, recoloured, relit, framed, turned through 360° and, for the whole skeleton, mirrored from one side. Render record.

Character imagery and native motion retain their separate project-enrolled asset rights; the open-anatomy license does not apply to them or to the website application code.
