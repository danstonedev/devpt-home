R&D

These DevPT projects are still in development. They are not released and are not for clinical use, and their previews may change or be unavailable. A project moves to the main DevPT catalogue when it is ready.

Under Pressure

A wireless sensor rig for cupping therapy. Each cup carries a small ESP32 board with pressure, temperature and humidity sensors. A gateway on the computer’s USB port collects the cups over ESP-NOW, and a browser app (Web Serial, Chrome or Edge) shows each cup live, records several cups on one clock and compares them.

In development. So far it has run only in simulation; it has not yet been tested on sensor hardware.

Open preview of Under Pressure

MASH

MASH (Motion Analysis Sensor Hub) is a separate research project: motion capture with wearable inertial (IMU) sensors. ESP32-S3 sensor nodes send their readings over ESP-NOW to a USB gateway, and a browser app (Web Serial, Chrome or Edge) shows the movement live in 3D, records sessions and exports the data.

In development. A research prototype, not for clinical use. Live data needs a MASH gateway connected over USB.
