Inside the rebuilt simLAB

The clinical picture.
Connected.

What you ask, find, and decide belongs to the same patient story. Explore the workspace from first conversation to class discussion.

01 / PATIENT ENCOUNTER

Meet the person
behind the case.

Interview an AI patient by text or voice. Read the record, explore symptoms, and choose the examinations that can help answer your clinical question.

  • A 3D patient in a clinical setting
  • Student-selected tests and recorded findings
  • Clinical instructor support during case practice
Enlarge ↗

02 / REASONING MAP

Show how the
evidence adds up.

Build a working differential. Position diagnoses by likelihood, flag what you must not miss, and connect the findings that support or challenge your thinking.

  • Supporting and disconfirming evidence
  • A visible map of the student's reasoning
  • A starting point for feedback and discussion
Enlarge ↗

03 / PLAN & FOLLOW-UP

Make a plan.
Then revisit it.

Link goals to findings, build a structured plan, and select in-clinic and home activities. Follow-up cases ask you to respond to adherence, progress, and changes in the patient's story.

  • Goals and interventions with a clinical rationale
  • Structured activity doses and home programs
  • Decisions to progress, regress, or change course
Enlarge ↗

04 / LIVE CLASS

Bring the class
into the conversation.

Faculty launch a session and students join with a class code. Reveal anonymous class patterns, compare sections, and use the debrief guide to discuss how the evidence shaped each decision.

  • Faculty-controlled shared-screen reveals
  • Anonymous class reasoning views
  • Live discussion around the same patient case

For students

A clinical visit,
with room to think.

Practice through instructor-selected cases. Carry the findings forward, make a defensible decision, and reflect on what you would do next.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Read the chart. Hear the patient's story. Decide what needs a closer look.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Choose your exam, record the findings, and connect the evidence to your differential.

  3. 03

    Act

    Document your reasoning. Build a plan and communicate with the care team where the case calls for it.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    Review feedback and discuss the decisions that should guide your next attempt.

For faculty

Teach the decisions.
See the thinking.

Bring simLAB into a live class or schedule case work for your learners. Keep the context, the evidence, and the teaching conversation together.

  • Organize your teachingManage groups, choose scenarios, and schedule practice or graded case work.
  • See the reasoningReview attempt records and class patterns to identify what deserves discussion.
  • Keep faculty judgment centralReview or enter grades and guide the debrief. Native DDx cases support formative feedback without an automatic score.
