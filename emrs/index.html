FOR DOCUMENTATION PRACTICE

simEMR

Build documentation skills using fictional patient records in a learning environment.

  • Practice writing evaluations and follow-up notes.
  • Connect examination findings, assessment and plan.

FOR TEACHING CLINICS

proBonoEMR

Support patient records, team documentation and faculty review in a clinic environment.

  • Work from a shared patient and appointment context.
  • Document the care provided by your team.
  • Review and co-sign through the clinical workflow.
