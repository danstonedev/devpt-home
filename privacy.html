Privacy

DevPT's public website introduces its products through information and interactive previews using fictional samples. The previews do not require an account.

Information you choose to send

If you contact DevPT by email, the information in that email is used to respond to your inquiry and coordinate demos, collaboration, or research conversations.

Website data

The public homepage is a static site. Hosting providers may process basic technical logs such as IP address, browser, time of request, and requested URL for security, diagnostics, and reliability.

Interactive previews

Product previews load from the product's own website when you open them. Practice note, schedule, feedback and pain-map changes stay within the sample and do not update patient or learner records.

The simLAB AI interview sends your questions and the demo conversation to its server and AI provider to produce replies. It uses a 30-minute demo session, short-lived conversation and reply-audio records for retries, and pseudonymous usage counters to enforce the three-exchange allowance and shared usage limits. The browser retains an opaque demo grant for that tab so reopening the preview resumes the same allowance.

Spoken input requires your action and microphone permission. Browser dictation may use an online speech service; spoken patient replies are generated from the AI reply. You can use text instead.

Student and patient data

Do not submit real patient data through the public website. DevPT classroom simulation is intended to use simulated cases and no real patient data.
